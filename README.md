# Night Watch

Your phone is a window into space. Everybody's phone looks into the same
sky, and enemy ships are hiding all around you — above, behind, under the
floor. Find them, call them out, shoot them. Hold your aim on one to lock
a missile. Keep the station alive as long as you can.

- **With a TV:** open the game on the TV, scan the code with each phone,
  point every phone at the TV and tap LOCK FRONT. The TV shows the whole
  battle from behind the players, with everybody's aim as a line in their
  colour, so people who are not playing can see what is going on.
- **Without a TV:** tap START A GAME ON THIS PHONE; friends scan the code
  on that phone. Agree on something in the room to call FRONT.

Best played on the floor or in swivel chairs.

**Bunker** is the second game, picked in the lobby: a small wooden bunker
in a graveyard at night, with Halloween monsters coming from every side —
pumpkinheads, skeletons, bats, ghosts that float through walls, brutes, and
a witch dropping pumpkin bombs. Turn to look, walk with the stick, switch
between a pistol, a shotgun and a sniper rifle with a scope, and hold
REPAIR at the walls they have battered. If one gets in, you lose. No
blood — they go poof.

**Haunted House** (`/haunt/`) is the bunker in your own living room. Print
a set of markers (`/haunt/print.html`), tape them round the room - on the
walls where monsters should break in, on the floor at doorways - and
everybody scans the code on the first page. Through the phone's camera the
walls open up into a graveyard, and the monsters come through the holes.
Walk over to a hole to board it back up. When the witch comes she tears
the roof off. Or switch to full immersion and the room is drawn instead.

See `HANDOFF.md` for how it works and `CLAUDE.md` for the checks to run
before shipping.

# Night Watch

Your phone is a window into space. Everybody's phone looks into the same
sky, and enemy ships are hiding all around you — above, behind, under the
floor. Find them, call them out, shoot them. Hold your aim on one to lock
a missile. Keep the station alive as long as you can.

- **With a TV:** open the game on the TV, scan the code with each phone,
  point every phone at the TV and tap LOCK FRONT. The TV shows the whole
  battle from behind the players, with everybody's aim as a line in their
  colour, so people who are not playing can see what is going on.
- **Without a TV:** tap START A GAME ON THIS PHONE; friends scan the code
  on that phone. Agree on something in the room to call FRONT.

Best played on the floor or in swivel chairs.

See `HANDOFF.md` for how it works and `CLAUDE.md` for the checks to run
before shipping.

Music and cues are rendered from the FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen
(CC BY 3.0) with `tools/music.py`. 3D by three.js (MIT, `web/lib/three-LICENSE`).
